Major UK pub chain closing all 106 sites as exact date revealed – see full list

Beefeater will be closing all 106 of its restaurants after 'significant cost increases' pushed owner Whitbread into a restructuring plan, placing around 3,800 jobs at risk

Comments
News
Zahra Khaliq News Reporter
13:04, 25 Jul 2026Updated 13:05, 25 Jul 2026

A major UK pub chain has announced it will be closing all 106 of its sites in the coming weeks.

‌

Beefeater, known for its 30-day aged steaks and mixed grills, confirmed it would be leaving the pub-restaurant industry for good on September 10.

‌

The closures are part of owner Whitbread's major restructuring plan to save £250million - which involves closing down its remaining Beefeater and Brewers Fayre sites, putting around 3,800 jobs at risk.

‌

Customers were told about the closure in a letter, seen by the Southend Echo, which was sent to Beefeater reward club members.

It said: "As you may have seen, we have recently announced changes to our business, which is resulting in the closure of our Branded Restaurants.

‌

"This means that on Thursday 10 September 2026 your local Beefeater and all other UK Beefeaters will close."

Whitbread boss Dominic Paul said in April that "significant cost increases" had pushed the firm into the overhaul.

A spokesperson for the business said it expects to be able to "retain a significant proportion of those affected" and will look into redeploying "as many of our impacted colleagues as possible," reports The Sun.
